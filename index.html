About

Surfaces, sensors, and the cells that meet them

I'm an MTech researcher at IIT Gandhinagar, working where biosensing meets engineered biomaterials. I design surfaces that capture and detect bacteria, build the chambers to test them, and read the results electrochemically.

Before that, I worked on enzyme-nanomaterial systems for environmental clean-up. I'm now looking for a PhD where I can bring this toolkit to living systems and engineered living materials.

Research

Three benches

MTech thesis · 2024 – present

Catching airborne bacteria on engineered surfaces

SMART Lab, IIT Gandhinagar · PI: Dr. Soumyadip Sett

How does the wettability of a surface change the way bacteria stick to it, and can that be used to capture airborne microbes for real-time monitoring?

  • Designed and built a microbiome simulation chamber that recreates atmospheric conditions, so capture can be tested in a controlled way. A joint patent was filed for it in 2025.
  • Developed substrates with nanostructures and molecularly imprinted polymers for selective bacterial capture and detection.
  • Developing electrochemical impedance spectroscopy as a real-time readout inside an integrated microfluidic platform. This part is in progress.
  • Supervised five undergraduate interns across two summer programmes.
  • wettability
  • molecularly imprinted polymers
  • nanostructures
  • impedance spectroscopy
  • microfluidics
  • bacterial culture

Project Fellow · 2021 – 2024

Enzymes on nanomaterials for environmental clean-up

Biochemistry Lab, IIT Gandhinagar · PI: Dr. Bhaskar Datta

Can enzymes and catalytic nanomaterials work together on pollutants and waste streams?

  • Synthesised and characterised nanoparticles, microspheres and hydrogels.
  • Built a urease-immobilised iron-oxide nanoconstruct to extract ammonia from urine, for circular remediation.
  • Studied laccase-mediator systems on phenolic and non-phenolic substrates.
  • Made copper oxide microspheres for catalytic dye degradation. This work was published in 2026.
  • Tested the antioxidant behaviour of Opuntia ficus-indica extracts using mammalian cell culture and analytical chemistry.
  • Supervised about 20 summer interns and MTech thesis students over two and a half years.
  • nanoparticles
  • hydrogels
  • enzyme immobilisation
  • mammalian cell culture
  • analytical chemistry
